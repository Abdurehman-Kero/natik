# Admin guide

One task per section. Sign in at the website with your phone number and password; you land on the Dashboard.

## Check payments (daily)

1. Open **Payments to check**. The oldest are first. A gold number in the menu means something is waiting.
2. Read the card: student, course, reference, and whether the amount **Matches**. Tap **View receipt** if needed.
3. **Confirm**: you have 5 seconds to press **Undo**. The student gets a text message and the course opens.
4. **Reject**: write the reason (it is texted to the student). They can send their details again.

## Record a cash payment

1. Payments to check › **Record cash payment**.
2. Type the student's name or phone, choose the course, check the amount and date, press **Record payment**.
3. The student has access straight away.

## Enrol, extend, renew or cancel

Open **Enrolments**. Filter by course, status or student. **Enrol student** gives access without payment.
In each row's ⋯ menu: **Extend access** (add days), **Renew** (expired courses), **Cancel enrolment** (needs a reason).
Progress and results are never deleted.

## Create a course

Courses › **New course**: title, language, level, summary, picture, free or paid with a price, days of access.
Assign teachers on the **Teachers** tab. Teachers add the lessons. When there is at least one published lesson,
press **Publish**.

Two course settings shape the learning flow: **How lessons open** (all open, or one after another: a lesson opens when
the lessons before it are done) and **Trailer video link** (a short YouTube or Vimeo video shown on the public course page).

## Edit the website

**Website content**: home headline, featured courses, About text, FAQ, student quotes, Privacy and Terms,
contact details and social links. Each block has **Use the standard wording**. Changes show within seconds.

## Messages and announcements

**Messages** has contact-form messages and "Report a problem" notes from students. Close them when done.
**Announcements**: choose Everyone, all students, or one course; post now or on a date.

## Reports and the activity log

**Reports**: choose a report, set the dates and course, then **Export CSV** (opens in Excel, Arabic names included).
**Activity log**: who did what and when. Filter by kind of action, person or date. Nothing in it can be edited.

## Certificates

**Certificates**: search by number, name or course. **Open PDF**, or **Cancel certificate** (reason required).
The verify page then says "cancelled". **Settings › Certificate signatory**: name, title and signature picture
printed on new certificates.

## Settings

Payment accounts (shown to students on the payment page), languages and levels, certificate signatory,
**SMS delivery** (retry failed texts), Admins and owner.

## People

**Users**: search, edit, send a password link, disable or enable, or **Delete personal data**. Only the Owner can
create or change admins.

## Chat with students and teachers

**Chats** (in the menu, with a number for unread messages) is the Natik team's shared inbox: every message a
student or teacher sends to "Natik team" arrives here, and any admin can answer. **New chat** searches a student
or teacher by name to write first. Send text, pictures, files (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, up to 20 MB) and
voice messages. Private chats between a student and their teacher are not visible to admins.
