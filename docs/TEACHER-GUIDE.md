# Teacher guide

Sign in with your phone number and password. **My courses** lists the courses an admin assigned to you.

## Build a lesson

1. Open a course › **Add unit**, then add a lesson to the unit.
2. In the lesson, add blocks: **text**, **video**, **PDF**, **audio** or **image**. Drag to reorder.
   Arabic text is typed right to left automatically; you can switch a paragraph's direction.
3. Settings on the right: **Free preview** (anyone can open it) and **Allow downloads**.
4. **Preview** shows exactly what students see. When ready, press **Publish**. **Hide** takes it down without losing results.

Limits: PDF and audio up to 25 MB, pictures up to 5 MB, video up to 100 MB in development (the video service in production).

## Make a guided lesson (steps, slides, video link)

In the lesson editor, the right column has **Lesson introduction**: a short description, what students will learn
(one goal per line) and the study time. Students read it first.

Each block has a **Step** menu: **Prepare**, **Watch**, **Revise**, **Examples**, or **Lesson** (no step). Students
walk through the steps one by one: Intro → Prepare → Lesson → Watch → Revise → Examples → Quiz. Steps with no
blocks are skipped, and a lesson with no steps looks exactly as before.

- **Slides** block: add slides one at a time with a title, a little text, a picture and a few key words. Reorder with the arrows.
- **Video link**: paste a YouTube or Vimeo link. The video stays on that site. Videos on other sites can be removed by their owners, so upload important videos instead.
- **Captions**: on an uploaded video, press **Add captions (.vtt)** to show subtitles. Students can pick the speed (0.75x to 2x), and the video continues where they stopped.
- **Always open**: if the course opens lessons in order, tick this to let students open that lesson at any time.

## Add a quiz to a lesson

1. In the lesson, the **Quiz** card › **Add quiz**. Tick "final quiz" for the course's one final quiz.
2. **Add question**: multiple choice, true / false, fill in the blank, or short answer. Set the marks.
3. Settings: pass mark, attempts, time limit, and whether students see answers afterwards.
4. **Preview**, then **Publish**. Passing a lesson quiz completes the lesson. The final quiz plus every lesson completes the course.

You can still edit questions later. Students who already started keep the version they began with.

## Mark short answers

**Marking** lists written answers, oldest first. Read the answer (your model answer is shown to you only),
type a score (halves allowed), add a comment, press **Save & next**. You can change a mark later.

## See how students are doing

Open the course › **Students**. Search by name. You see progress, lessons done, quiz average, final quiz and last
active, and **Export CSV**. Phone numbers and payments are never shown to teachers.
Open **Quiz attempts** for one student to give **one extra attempt** on a quiz.

## Announce to a course

**Announcements** › choose your course › write › post now or on a date.

## Chat

**Messages** lets you chat with the students of your courses and with the Natik team. Students start a chat from
their side; you can also press **New chat** to write to a student of your course. You can send text, pictures,
files and voice messages (hold the microphone, then send).
